OUR IMPERIAL HERITAGE

The Grand Revival of India's Royal Dastarkhwans

❖ ❖ ❖

SAVORIA was conceived as a sacred homage to the legendary culinary courts of Awadh, Mewar, Rampur, and the Malabar coast.

Under the stewardship of Master Ustad Auguste Moreau, our kitchen revives ancient slow-cooking secrets (Dum Pukht) where fragrant yakhni broths, cold-pressed stone spices, and organic A2 ghee marry contemporary Michelin-starred finesse.

Slow Dum Pukht Alchemy

Sealed earthen handis cooked on low charcoal heat to trap pure botanical aromatics.

Single-Estate Indian Spices

Pampore saffron, Guntur chillies, Malabar tellicherry pepper, and Kannauj rose waters.
